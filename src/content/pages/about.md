## Why This Blog?

I started this blog because I believe the best way to truly understand something is to explain it simply. Most people use computers every day without knowing what happens when they press a key, send a packet, or load a webpage.

This blog is my attempt to bridge that gap. I write about:

- 🔧 **How hardware actually works** — RAM, CPUs, SSDs, and the silicon beneath your fingertips
- 🌐 **Networking from the ground up** — packets, protocols, NAT, DNS, and everything in between
- 🏗️ **Backend & system design** — building things that scale, and understanding why they work
- 🔐 **Security & systems** — DRM, encryption, and the invisible infrastructure of the internet

Every post aims to take a complex topic and make it click. No jargon walls. No hand-waving. Just clear explanations with enough depth to actually be useful.
